Receiver dependence

The same transverse history direction is much more unusual to the value head than to the policy head. At |λ|=2, real value-logit RMS sensitivity is about 1.88× the mean matched-control sensitivity, association change is 1.32×, and policy-logit RMS is only 1.12×.

What the three positions did

At |λ|=2 the real direction's value-logit percentile was 92.4%, 92.4%, and 98.5% across the starting, early-game, and king-only positions. The association-layer percentiles were 71.2%, 89.4%, and 98.5%. The endgame position also reached the 92.4th percentile for policy-logit sensitivity.

In the starting position, +2 changed the winning value bin from 43 to 37 while leaving the selected move unchanged; at +4 it changed both the value bin and the selected move. In the second position, +4 changed the value bin without changing the selected move. That remains the clearest current example of two frozen heads reading the same altered latent state differently.

Current reading

The orthogonal temporal component is tiny in norm, but it is not behaving like a generic matched direction. In this three-position smoke test it is consistently high-ranked for value-head sensitivity and moderately high-ranked at the shared association layer. The policy head is less special. This supports a larger held-out position test; it does not yet establish chess-strength improvement or a general biological mechanism.

Numerical issue closed

The first smoke run exposed a float32 Jensen–Shannon underflow on subnormal softmax tails. JS is now evaluated in float64 log space. The rerun at the fixed implementation head produced finite JS aggregates at all three magnitudes and reproduced the pre-softmax specificity pattern.

The gate

r⊥ = r − ((r · h) / (h · h + ε)) h

Each shuffled control is permuted, re-orthogonalized against h, and rescaled to ||r⊥||. Every real/control direction is evaluated at both +λ and −λ; the larger effect is retained, preventing sign cherry-picking.
