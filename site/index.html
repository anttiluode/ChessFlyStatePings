Internal next-step targeting

The probe reads a frozen receiver. It does not feed its response back into recurrence or test better chess play. Twelve cue pairs share four common positions; these are descriptive measurements.

The first attempt is preserved

Version 1 formed the ping after two steps and was inconclusive. The step-1 readout was zero, making the two-step residue parallel to current activity. Orthogonalization removed it, leaving only rounding residue.

Version 2 was declared before its run: wait for the first noncollinear prefix at step 3, deliver at step 4, and hold out step 5. Cases, controls and thresholds stayed fixed.

Gate 3: receiver-query geometry

Instead of treating the raw orthogonal history vector as a generic address, Gate 3 asks what that direction does to the frozen learned receiver. For association map A, current state h, history direction r⊥ and magnitude a:

g(a) = [A(h + a r⊥) − A(h − a r⊥)] / (2a)

Step 4 is the stored memory signature; step 5 is the query signature. Thirty-two matched controls apply the same feature permutation at both steps, then re-orthogonalize and norm-match before passing through the same frozen receiver.

Association versus value geometry

Association-space enrichment is present but weaker: the real correct-margin percentile is 83.3% at magnitude 1, then falls to 71.2% and 62.1%. The value-head effect is much more stable across scale. This repeats the receiver-dependence seen in Gate 1b from a different measurement direction.

same temporal direction ≠ same geometry under every receiver

The three-gate split

Gate 1b: the orthogonal history direction is unusually readable by some frozen heads.

Gate 2: the raw direction is not a useful generic cosine key to its own predecessor.

Gate 3: the learned receiver still does not create successful recall, but the value geometry makes the real temporal signature much more predecessor-specific than matched directions.

Reading at Gate 3

The hypothesis was relational: a history-bearing state may become query-like with respect to a learned receiver geometry. That n=3 result was suggestive. The later same-present assay above finds history-dependent responses but fails its native-continuation targeting gate.
